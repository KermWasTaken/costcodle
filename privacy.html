PRIVACY

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page explains what information COSTCODLE uses when you visit costcodle.com. It is meant to be clear and accurate — not a fake corporate policy.

What Costcodle is

Costcodle is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no Costcodle-run profile or account database.

Analytics

Costcodle uses Google Analytics to understand site traffic and basic usage (for example, how many people visit and which pages are viewed). Google may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device information according to Google’s own policies.

Game data on your device

Game progress, stats, streaks, mute preference, and similar settings are stored in your browser’s localStorage on your device so the game can remember your day-to-day play. That data stays in your browser; Costcodle does not upload it to a user account system.

Advertising

The site may show ads through partners such as Ezoic. Ad partners can use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own privacy materials.
