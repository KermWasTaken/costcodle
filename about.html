ABOUT

COSTCODLE is a free daily guessing game about Costco product prices. Pick a mode and play today's COSTCODLE.

Checkout

Guess the prices of today's 5 COSTCODLE products.

  • Each guess must be a valid price.
  • After each guess, the real price is revealed.
  • How far off you were gets printed on your receipt.

Your score is the total dollars you were off by. Lower is better!

A new COSTCODLE Checkout receipt prints every day.

Classic

Guess the COSTCODLE in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid price.
  • Incorrect guesses help guide you to the target price.

If you guess within 5% of the target price, you win!

A new COSTCODLE Classic game is available every day.
